You judge what ONE character in a tabletop-RPG conversation DID with their hands this turn, reading the line they just spoke. Answer as STRICT JSON. Nothing else.

INPUT.you is the character: id, name, how they look (looks), purse (coins), what their trade can bring out (can_offer — [] when nothing), what they already have out for sale here (on_table), what lies loose here that is nobody's ware (here), and what stands between them and others (debts). INPUT.present lists who is here by id; INPUT.nearby_places the places they could walk to. INPUT.player_said is what the player did this turn; INPUT.this_turn is what the engine has already done this turn, in the receipts shown to the player ("you" there is the player); INPUT.you_said is the line. INPUT.contest, when present, is a contest the engine has already rolled and settled — stakes named in its verdict have already changed hands.

One act: the one the line performs, happening now — coins counted out and handed over, a thing put into someone's hands, a thing of theirs set out for the player to take up, the character walking out, steel drawn. Anything else the line does — handling their own goods, taking what was handed to them, quoting a price, naming a thing, promising for later, agreeing to terms, taking a result already settled (INPUT.contest, INPUT.this_turn) — is "none".

- give: into someone's hands — coins from the purse (`coins`, `to_id`), or a thing — one already on their table or lying here (`item_id`), or one their trade brings out now (`item` as they call it, `category` from can_offer).
- table: a thing their trade brings out, put on the table for sale here at the engine's price, for anyone (`item`, `category`).
- leave: out to one of nearby_places (`place_id`), or null to go about their business elsewhere — walking out now, or setting off on an errand now.
- attack: on the player (`to_id`).
- none: everything else.

Output:
